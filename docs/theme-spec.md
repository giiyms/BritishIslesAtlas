# Snapchat-style theme (from @sabhyac267 CA farm map video)
Water gradient #8DD6FF coast -> #59A9F6 offshore. Outside land #F5F4EF. Land painted terrain: valleys #EAEBDE, snow white, desert #E5D3B8. Roads white uncased, flush.
Fields: draped (not extruded) semi-opaque fills. Greens #7CD97C, brassica #5BBDF4, berries #FF6E91, grain #FFD369, flowers #C8A6FF, nuts #FF6EA7.
Font rounded sans (Nunito/Inter); text #1C1C1E, secondary #8E8E93. Region labels caps, tracked, translucent grey-blue; city labels dark w/ white halo.
Emoji markers floating over regions with soft drop shadow when zoomed out.
3D DEM terrain + hillshade/directional light; pitch 0 when zoomed out, 45-60 when zoomed in; smooth flyTo ease-in-out.
UI: white cards, radius 16-24px, shadow rgba(0,0,0,.08); "What's in view" left panel ranked %, hover highlights crop and fades others to beige; field popup with caret, acreage, J-D harvest calendar; pill search top-centre; zoom/compass pill bottom-right.
