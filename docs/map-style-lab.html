1 · Heatmaps: what ships, the textbook version, and the trap

Real data from the repo: 8,667 OpenStreetMap pharmacies inside the 632 GB seats. Drag the radius to see why pixel-radius heatmaps are not comparable across zooms.

2 · Better than a heatmap: bin, normalise, measure

Same points, aggregated into shapes that carry real units. The 2024 Westminster seats were drawn to an electorate quota of 69,724–77,062. So a count per seat is already roughly a count per 73,000 voters, and the seat file you ship gives you normalisation for free. Hover any seat.

3 · Seats: from one colour to a real election map

The voting layer shows 631 seats in one colour, which tells the reader almost nothing. The repo already contains Electoral Calculus seat projections (polls-seats.json). These panels use that data, under conventions from BBC, FT and Sky election maps. Hover the hexes.

4 · Basemap treatments: same data, six moods

Choose the basemap that suits the overlay. Light neutrals suit choropleths, dark suits glow and density, and Toner suits print. Token swatches are listed under each card.

5 · UI: a layer panel that explains itself

This proposal replaces 50 equal-weight chips with three roles: one surface (areal or continuous, pick exactly one), up to five point layers, and one basemap. Every active layer brings its own legend, built from the same config that writes the paint expression.

Data: OSM extract 2026‑10‑05 (ODbL) · ONS PCON 2024 (OGL) · Electoral Calculus projection 2026‑10‑05. Sources & method
  1. One surface at a time. Two choropleths or a heatmap on a choropleth can't be read. A radio group enforces this, so the map stays legible without written rules.
  2. Cap point layers at 5. That's about the limit for categorical colour plus icon shape. A sixth layer prompts "replace which?" instead of adding more soup.
  3. Legend = config. Generate the step/interpolate expression and the legend from one object {stops, colors, unit, source}, so they can never drift apart.
  4. Live counts in view (querySourceFeatures on moveend). This turns a pretty map into an answer: "38 hospitals here".
  5. Move the voting editorial out of the catalogue. Give it its own mode or route with its own explainer. Mixing a partisan guide into a reference atlas makes every other layer look less trustworthy.
  6. Mobile gets a bottom sheet with three detents. The peek detent shows only the active surface's legend.
